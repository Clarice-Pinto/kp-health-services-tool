# Key Populations-Sensitive Health Services and Programming

An interactive orientation and planning tool for health services for key populations, aligned with WHO normative guidance.

## Key populations covered
- Men who have sex with men (MSM)
- Trans and gender diverse people (TGD)
- Sex workers (SW)
- People who inject drugs (PWID)
- People in prisons and other closed settings (PPC)

## Features
- Interactive ecosystem diagram with population selection
- WHO essential services checklist with downloadable formats
- 36 WHO recommendations and guidance across 6 topic areas
- Situation analysis with disease burden, barriers, stigma, and gender/age factors
- Service delivery models, care pathways, community engagement, PHC integration
- Monitoring indicators aligned with GAM 2026
- 48 Vancouver-style references with links to WHO publications
- Downloadable page content for offline workshop use

## How to use
Open `index.html` in any browser. Click a population circle, then navigate tabs.

## Disclaimer
This tool consolidates existing WHO normative guidance. It does not introduce new recommendations or clinical protocols.
